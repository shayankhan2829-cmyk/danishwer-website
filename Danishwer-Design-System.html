Design Philosophy

Danishwer serves people in moments of real need. The design reflects that: no distractions, no tricks, no visual noise. Trust is earned through clarity and consistency. Every component choice prioritizes comprehension and conversion over aesthetic novelty. The one exception is the typography pairing, which is deliberately characterful: Fraunces at large sizes carries emotional weight and tells people this organization has a personality, not just a policy.

🎯

Purposeful

Every element earns its place. If removing it doesn't break comprehension, it shouldn't be there.

🤝

Trustworthy

Clean structure, honest copy, no dark patterns. The design communicates credibility at every touchpoint.

📱

Accessible

Mobile-first, minimum 44px touch targets, WCAG AA contrast, full prefers-reduced-motion support.

Do / Don't

These are the rules that prevent the design from drifting into generic territory. They are specific enough to be enforced.

Do

  • Use Fraunces for all display headlines
  • Use the primary gradient on CTA buttons
  • Apply --blue for education, --green for healthcare
  • Verify every statistic before publishing it
  • Use 44px minimum touch targets on all tappable elements
  • Include prefers-reduced-motion on every animation
  • Use inline SVG with Lucide style for all icons
  • Apply glass morphism only to the nav pill

Don't

  • Don't use purple (#7C3AED) anywhere, ever
  • Don't add particle.js or canvas animations
  • Don't use stock photos with poverty aesthetics
  • Don't place placeholder numbers on the live site
  • Don't pre-check recurring donation checkboxes
  • Don't use auto-playing video in the hero
  • Don't use pop-ups or interstitials in the apply flow
  • Don't mix icon styles (Lucide-only policy)
